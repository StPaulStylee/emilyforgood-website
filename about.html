Background

A little about me.

I have about 15 years of marketing experience, moving from smaller marketing roles into strategic direction. During that time I've worked for clients across industries, including retail, healthcare, financial services, and customer experience. I've been recognized for developing marketing programs that resulted in brand expansion, customer acquisition, and increased revenue.

I've since stepped out on my own to support small, local businesses, leaving the corporate world to offer my expertise to businesses I care about. Businesses like yours!

Outside of my business, I'm a mom to some very energetic kids (and a couple of fur babes). I enjoy spending my (very little) free time cooking, dancing, listening to all kinds of music, working out, and acting.

I was raised in the DC area and moved to Minnesota in 2012. Having traded in a frantic East Coast life for a slower-paced Midwest existence, I possess a tenacity and exuberance coupled with a patience and resilience that only a once-east-coaster-turned-midwesterner (and working mom of three!) could espouse. Qualities that have worked well for me in my career, and will work well for you as my client.
